MiniMax H3 / FastH3 - REFERENCE (SUBJECT CONSISTENCY) PROMPT GUIDE
Geekatplay Studio distillation of the official MiniMax H3 reference prompting rules.
Drop the official ref-en.txt here to override this file.

1. SUBJECT TAGS
Every recurring character, creature, vehicle or prop gets a numbered tag:
   <Subject 1>, <Subject 2>, <Subject 3> ...
Tags are assigned once, in a Character Bible, and never renumbered mid-story.

2. CHARACTER BIBLE ENTRY
One entry per subject, written as a single descriptive clause that repeats verbatim
in every segment where the subject appears:
   <Subject 1>: Dr. Maya Vance, a woman in her late twenties, braided auburn hair,
   beige utility jumpsuit with rolled sleeves, amber safety visor pushed up on her forehead.
Lock: age, build, hair, face, clothing, colours, accessories. Do not restyle between shots.
Lighting, expression, pose and camera distance are free to change; identity is not.

3. USING TAGS IN A SHOT
First mention in a shot carries the full description, later mentions use the tag alone:
   "[Shot 1] <Subject 1> Dr. Maya Vance, braided auburn hair and beige utility jumpsuit,
   kneels beside the glowing seed tray... <Subject 2> hovers just above her shoulder."

4. VOICE IDENTITY
Each speaking subject gets a stable speaker tag: (S1), (S2), ...
The tag binds to the subject for the whole story so the voice does not change between segments:
   "<Subject 1> (S1) says: <d>[English] It is still warm.</d>"
Describe the voice once in the bible (pitch, age, accent, delivery) and never contradict it.

5. CONTINUITY BETWEEN SEGMENTS
- Carry the environment forward: same location, time of day and weather unless the story cuts away.
- End a segment on a stable pose and start the next one from that pose.
- Keep props in the hand or place they ended in.
- Re-state the style clause ("cinematic live-action, anamorphic, warm key light") in every segment.

6. REFERENCE MEDIA
When reference images or videos are supplied, map them to the same tags
(ref_image_1 -> <Subject 1>) and keep the mapping stable for the whole story.
Reference frames must be at least 5 frames long and are resized to the 32 px canvas grid.
