MiniMax H3 / FastH3 - BASE PROMPT GUIDE (text-to-audio-video)
Geekatplay Studio distillation of the official MiniMax H3 base prompting rules.
Drop the official base-en.txt here to override this file.

1. STRUCTURE
Write one continuous, present-tense paragraph per shot. Order the information as:
   subject -> subject action -> environment -> camera motion -> lighting/atmosphere -> style.
Never write bullet lists, never write instructions to the model ("make sure to..."),
never use second person. Describe only what the camera can see and the mic can hear.

2. SHOTS AND CUTS
A segment opens with an establishing shot written as:
   [Shot 1] <description>
A mid-segment cut is written with an explicit timestamp:
   [Shot 2] At 00:02.600, the camera cuts to <shot size> of <subject>, <action>.
Timestamps use MM:SS.mmm. Keep at most two shots per 5-second segment.

3. CAMERA VOCABULARY
Use the standard verbs: push in, pull back, pan left/right, tilt up/down,
track forward/backward, orbit left/right, crane up/down, handheld follow, static.
Qualify every motion with amplitude (small / medium / large) and speed (slow / medium / fast):
   "The camera tracks forward with large amplitude at fast speed alongside <Subject 1>."
Shot sizes: extreme wide, wide, medium wide, medium, medium close-up, close-up, extreme close-up.

4. AUDIO
Native audio is generated with the video. Always describe it.
   Foley / ambience: concrete physical sounds in the frame ("dry sand crunching under paws,
   gulls calling offshore, a low ocean swell").
   Dialogue: <speaker tag> says: <d>[English] <line></d>
   Keep a line under ~12 words for a 5-second shot; one speaker per shot is safest.
   Non-diegetic music: describe instrumentation, tempo and where it swells or drops out.

5. HARD CONSTRAINTS
- 24 fps. Frame counts must follow the 17k + 5 grid (5, 22, 39, ..., 124, 141, ...).
- Canvas width and height must be multiples of 32, long edge capped at 1344.
- No on-screen text unless requested; if requested, quote it exactly and spell it correctly.
- No dissolves or fades unless explicitly asked for - cuts land hard.
- Do not repeat a name or a job title twice in the same shot.

6. NEGATIVE PHRASING
Put prohibitions at the end of the prompt as a short clause, e.g.
"no soft dissolves, no subtitle bars, no on-screen watermarks."
